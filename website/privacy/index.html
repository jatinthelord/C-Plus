Privacy and account security

What is public

Your username, display name, bio, avatar, published wiki posts, and any final public suspension record may be visible.

What stays private

Email address, first and last name, birth date, password data, internal reports, and moderation evidence are not published.

Cookies and sessions

The site stores an essential cookie named csp_cookie_choice for one year. Supabase stores the authenticated session in browser storage. The site does not use advertising or tracking cookies.

Passwords

Passwords are processed by Supabase Auth and are never stored in this website repository. Use the account page to request a password-reset email.
